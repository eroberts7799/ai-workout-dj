## Problem Statement

AWDJ nails WHEN (drops land 97% on-time vs real interval boundaries) and is learning WHAT (taste + energy fit). Two things are still missing for the version that gives Ethan chills: the run's TERRAIN is not part of the choreography plan (crest rewards are reactive, not anticipated), and the start ritual, while much better after build 31, is not yet fully invisible. This is a personal instrument — the bar is Ethan's own chills, nobody else's.

## What Makes This Cool

- The conductor reads tomorrow's route like it reads tomorrow's workout: the 2k climb at mile 4 gets its buildup started early, the summit drop is inevitable, the final mile gets the anthem.
- "Delegated intent": the machine decided before you woke up. Wake, run, everything scored.
- The N=1 inversion (session eureka): every music+fitness product died on licensing, onboarding, and generic data. A personal instrument can demand 9 years of listening history, tomorrow's GPX, and per-song HR response — an emotional map no mass product can structurally build.

## Constraints

- Spotify tier physics: one sealed DRM stream, jump cuts only; owned files carry the crossfade/texture magic. Parity law (2026-09-01): every feature lands on both tiers at the same level.
- Watch tier is boundary-mode only (CIQ platform physics).
- Engine parity law: TS is source of truth; Swift port ships same-session or gets a PARITY.md ledger line.
- No structured runs until ~2026-09-08; the 10-min shakeout test is staged on the Garmin calendar.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Terrain in the replay lab (minimal viable) — CHOSEN FIRST
GPX ingest → TerrainCue extraction (climbStart / crest / descent / finalMile with predicted times ± confidence) → backtest against the 41-run corpus using the same harness that proved the interval brain. Effort S (CC: ~1 evening). Zero live risk. Output: crest-prediction-error-seconds, the number that decides everything downstream. Reuses: `scripts/backtest.ts`, `GradeTracker`, `analysis/extract_structured_runs.py` corpus, altitude streams already in every session log.

### Approach B: The route-aware conductor (ideal architecture) — CHOSEN SECOND, GATED
Full loop: tomorrow's route auto-published beside next-workout (the workout pipeline's pattern: Mac pull script → Vercel Blob → key-gated relay endpoint → phone auto-fetch at app open); terrain cues merged into LiveEngine's anticipation (ETA machinery re-targeted at climbs/crests); pace-over-grade model from the corpus; hindsight re-choreography diff after every run feeding the emotional map; ready-state card shows route + workout. Effort L (CC: ~1-2 wks). Phone tiers get it by construction (engine-level, satisfies the parity law); the watch tier honors terrain cues at song boundaries only — a deliberate reduced surface per the existing PARITY.md watch section, ledgered there, not a parity violation ("the song after the crest is the reward"). TerrainCue confidence contract: cues with confidence <0.5 stay reactive-only (today's crest rules); ≥0.5 may pre-commit a buildup, with the engine's existing mid-build re-aim correcting live drift. Confidence is computed (provisionally) from three inputs: pace-model residual for the cue's grade bucket (per-bucket std from the corpus fit — the dominant term), elevation quality of the route segment (step-0 criterion), and GPX point density across the cue window; A's backtest calibrates the exact weighting before B consumes it. Gates: (1) Approach A's crest error passes the bar, (2) the live shakeout validates the interval brain, (3) the route-source probe (Open Questions) lands.

### Approach C: Hindsight studio first (creative/lateral) — DEFERRED, NOT REJECTED
Post-run re-choreograph diff as the data engine; trains the emotional map from every run including easy ones. Strong second move after A; folds into B's later stages. **This is where premise 2's second magic item (per-song HR-response learning) lives**: the hindsight diff is its labeled-data source, and the existing HR-response scoring (distance-aligned command-log × HR-log transitions, running since 8/24) keeps accumulating in the background meanwhile. No further HR-model work is scoped in this document — C's design doc will own it, gated behind A.

## Recommended Approach

**A then B** (Ethan's own sequencing; decision id 8302a460 in the project decision log). A is the first slice of B; it runs entirely offline during the no-structured-runs week and converts "is route choreography real?" from a vibe into a number. B's go-gate = A's crest error bar + shakeout green.

Proposed bar for A (provisional, mirrors the interval gate): crest cues within ≤5s p90, ≤10s max, on the constant-effort subset of the corpus — runs whose FIT workout messages carry no prescriptive hard steps (easy/long runs). A scores TWO variants: (1) pure pre-run prediction (GPX + corpus pace-over-grade model only) and (2) live-drift-corrected (the engine's existing ETA re-aim applied to terrain cues during replay). If (1) misses but (2) passes, B proceeds with drift correction mandatory; if both miss, B's live wiring waits and C (hindsight studio) becomes the next move. Step 0 of A is an elevation-data-quality audit of the corpus. Provisional cleanliness criterion (GUESS until the audit calibrates it): a run is clean if barometric altitude is present and the median absolute per-second altitude delta outside detected climbs is <1.5m. The bar requires ≥10 clean runs with real elevation; fewer means the audit widens the corpus (Strava-synced activities) before A is judged.

**Invisibility status:** the zero-tap ready state shipped in build 31 (auto library, auto workout, keep-alive, watch-only start). The one remaining invisibility item is route auto-publish joining the existing 05:00 launchd job — it is PART of Approach B, not a separate workstream.

**Fallback rule (all stages):** no planned route, missing GPX, or elevation service down → degrade to current behavior exactly (plan-only choreography, reactive crest rules). Terrain cues are additive; their absence must never change today's product.

## Open Questions

- Route source for planned runs: Garmin Courses on calendar? Strava routes? A drawn GPX dropped in a folder? (Pull-script probe needed — same pattern as the workout pull.)
- Pace-over-grade: fit globally or per-effort-level? The corpus has both easy and hard runs.
- Crest cue vs. Runna plan conflict on structured runs: the engine already suppresses reactive crest rewards near a planned hard step (the CREST_MIN_ETA guard: no crest reward within 45s of a hard-step ETA — the plan owns the moment). Confirm pre-planned terrain cues inherit the identical rule.
- Watch tier expression: boundary-mode can honor terrain cues only at song ends — is "next song after the crest is the reward" good enough there?

## Success Criteria

- A: crest-prediction error ≤5s p90 / ≤10s max on ≥10 corpus runs with real elevation; report auto-generated like the interval backtest.
- B (later): one real run where the climb's buildup starts before the climb and Ethan reports chills without having touched the phone that morning.
- Invisible: zero taps between waking and running on a planned-workout day (Spotify wake excepted).
